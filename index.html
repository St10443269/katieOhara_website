About Me

Katie O'Hara

I am a 21 year old, third year BA Interior Design student at Emeris, Vega Pretoria, with a strong passion for creating thoughtful, functional and meaningful spaces. For me, interior design is about more than creating visually beautiful environments; it is about understanding people and designing spaces that positively influence the way they live, work, interact and connect.

I was drawn to interior design because it allows me to combine creativity with purpose. I enjoy exploring how colour, materiality, texture, lighting, form and spatial planning can work together to transform an environment and create a particular experience.

My approach to design is strongly influenced by nature and biophilic principles. I enjoy working with natural materials, organic forms, textures and earthy colour palettes to create interiors that feel warm, authentic and connected to their environment.

I see myself as a creative and developing designer who is eager to continue learning through both academic and professional experience. Ultimately, I aspire to create interiors that people do not simply see, but experience: spaces that feel considered, welcoming and memorable.

Personal brand identity

KOH Studios is a client-focused interior design firm built on the belief that successful spaces respond to the individual needs, lifestyle and personality of each client. The identity pairs a refined, light serif wordmark with a sunset-over-water emblem, drawn in terracotta, sage and deep blue.

Design philosophy

Designing beyond aesthetics, creating spaces that connect, inspire and belong.

My approach is rooted in the connection between people, spaces and nature. Through thoughtful spatial planning, natural materials, expressive forms and carefully selected details, I create environments that balance beauty, functionality and wellbeing. Every space should tell a story, reflect its purpose and positively influence the people who experience it.
